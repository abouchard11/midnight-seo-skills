# Support — Midnight GEO Pro Pack

Email: **support@midnightdev.dev**

This is what a paid seat includes. Nothing else is implied.

## Included

- **Email support** from support@midnightdev.dev for questions on applying the
  skills in this pack to your own properties.
- **One clarification round per buyer.** One thread, one round of answers
  after you have actually tried the skill. Not an ongoing retainer.
- **Updates for 12 months** from the date on your Stripe receipt: you get
  the next pack zips (same seat, same license) if a new version ships in
  that window.

## Not included

- No site audits (we will not run `/geo`, `/geo-crawl`, or GSC against your
  domain for you).
- No calls, screenshares, or Slack.
- No custom skill writing, no implementation work, no “can you just look
  at this URL.”
- No ranking, citation, or traffic guarantees. These are operator
  playbooks, not a service engagement.

## How to write

Subject: `GEO Pro — <your domain> — <skill name>`

Attach the Stripe receipt. Paste the skill output or the error, not a
screenshot of the whole IDE. If the question is “how do I start,” read
`commercial/INSTALL.md` first — that is not a clarification round.

## Updates

When a new zip exists, email support@midnightdev.dev with the original
receipt and the version you have. Delivery is by email (the GitHub repo
is private). The 12-month window does not extend the license grant in
`LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`; it only covers pack-file updates.
